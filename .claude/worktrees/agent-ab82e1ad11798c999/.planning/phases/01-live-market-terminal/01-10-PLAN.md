<objective>
Build the live watchlist panel components: rows whose prices stream and flash, with up/down markers, a colored Day %, sparklines that fill in from page load, selection styling, and the loading, empty and error states.

Purpose: this is the core "prices stream and flash" moment of the demo (UI-02) and the frontend half of WATCH-01. The panel is built and tested standalone here; plan 01-12 places it in the page and owns the selection. Implements D-05, D-07, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12 (row styling), D-13, and the OQ-1/OQ-2 resolutions.
Output: `useWatchlist`, `WatchlistPanel`, `WatchlistRow`, `PriceCell`, `Sparkline`, `lib/sparkline.ts`, tests.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim, not in user-story form; see plan 01-01): User starts the app with one script, opens http://localhost:8000, and watches the 10 default tickers stream live with flashing prices, sparklines and a main chart, next to a $10,000 portfolio.

## UI Considerations (from 01-UI-SPEC.md) handled in this plan

Covered and lifted into must_haves: watchlist empty, loading, error, partial (null price), zero-one-many, overflow, long-text; connection error (muted prices, suppressed flash); sparkline partial (fewer than 2 points / flat series). Outside market hours in Massive mode prices are static, so there is no flash and sparklines are flat muted lines; that is expected (PLAN section 13.2 item 14), not a defect.
